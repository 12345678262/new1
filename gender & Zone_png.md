## 📝 Conclusion

Based on the Zomato sales data analysis:

1.  **Zone Analysis:** Central zone is the top revenue generator, followed by Western and Northern zones. Southern zone has the lowest revenue, indicating a need for marketing focus there.

2.  **Gender Analysis:** Female customers are the primary buyers with 60% contribution compared to 40% Male. This shows females have higher purchasing power in food ordering.

3.  **Business Insight:** The company should focus more offers and campaigns targeting female customers in the Central zone to maximize profit.

4.  **Future Scope:** Adding Age and Occupation analysis can give even deeper insights into customer behavior.
